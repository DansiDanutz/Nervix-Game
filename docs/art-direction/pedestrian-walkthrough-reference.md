# Pedestrian walkthrough reference (supporting, not a target)

Source: "Cinque Terre - Environment Walkthrough" by Sheila A (@Sheila_3D),
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZaIVvEqhzHY. Third-party work, used only as a private study
reference. Frames are kept locally in `docs/references/external/cinque-terre-walkthrough/`
(gitignored, never committed or shipped). Do not copy its buildings, layout or textures.

A01-plaza stays the controlling target. This video shows how a stylised Mediterranean
environment reads **at walking eye level in real time**, which A01 (a raised painting) cannot.

Lessons to apply:
1. **Camera**: about 1.5–1.7 m eye height, level gaze, about 60° vertical FOV. Facades fill the
   frame sides; sky only shows above and between roofs. Nothing hangs across the view.
2. **Enclosure**: streets are 4–8 m wide with 3–5 storey facades on both sides, so there is no void
   and no flat horizon on the street. The square opens up after the street (`key_012`, `key_015`).
3. **Fountain**: a tiered stone fountain on a raised circular platform with a contrasting ring
   border. It reads instantly because the ground around it is open and lighter (`key_012`,
   `key_015`).
4. **Shapes**: chunky bevelled edges, slightly irregular stone, deep window reveals, shutters,
   balconies, arches, stairs. No knife-edge boxes.
5. **Materials**: saturated warm plaster (terracotta, ochre, rose) with soft value variation;
   painted cobble and paving; green or teal shutters. For Nervix, use A01's palette (cream stone,
   teal, plum, gold) in place of Cinque Terre's red and yellow.
6. **Light**: one strong warm sun with soft shadows, plus a bright blue sky with soft clouds for
   fill. Shadowed sides stay readable (sky fill), never black. Mild fog only at distance.
7. **Props**: pots, planters, crates, benches, café tables, umbrellas, produce stands and lanterns
   along walls at human scale. They make the street feel inhabited without blocking sightlines.

Key frames (local only): key_001 and key_002 (street enclosure at eye level), key_004 (shopfronts and
produce), key_006 (open terrace), key_012 and key_015 (fountain square), key_019 (square from a
street mouth), key_029 (market stalls in front of a café).
