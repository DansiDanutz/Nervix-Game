# Nervix World
A NervixPay user enters a walkable Mediterranean merchant city at street level, facing Fountain Square. A01 in [the reference gallery](assets/references/README.md) is the opening camera and style target. Use the mouse to look and interact, and the keyboard to walk. The player discovers businesses by following the streets and entering their doors.

A signed-up free user can explore. A completed account can discover businesses according to existing eligibility. A monthly subscriber can build a business house only when also verified as a merchant. Only that merchant can edit their house, welcome, decor, products, app and website. Visitors cannot publish content. Load business identity and catalogs from records at runtime.

OpenPoker is the platform building. DanGG is one independent business with its own room and app; other builders have separate houses, catalogs and permissions. Use fixtures for development. No real payments or wagers during testing.
