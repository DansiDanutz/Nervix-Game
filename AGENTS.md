# For assistants

- Read `PROGRESS.md` first; it is the live status. Update it after each meaningful milestone.
- The specification is `docs/MASTER_PROMPT.md`. Asset rules are `docs/ASSETS.md`.
- Blender is the visual source of truth. Never claim a `.blend` or export exists without running Blender and verifying the file.
- Automation touches only the `NERVIX_MANAGED` collection. Hand-edited assets are never deleted or rewritten.
- No secrets, credentials or customer records in `.blend` files, exports or git.
- Payments: server-side only. A redirect or an agent statement is never proof of payment.
- Merchant data (names, logos, catalogs, ad creatives) loads at runtime; never bake it into the city.
- Commit small, verified steps. `main` is merge-only once the repo is shared.
